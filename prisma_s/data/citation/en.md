If you use this tool or its outputs in published or shared work, please cite:

**The tool**
> Wielgosz, B. (2026). *PRISMA-S Lit Review* [Computer software].
> https://github.com/wielgosz/PRISMA_S_Lit_Report

**The associated publication**
> Wielgosz, B., dos Santos, A. B., Carter, S., Berger, A., Schneider, M.,
> Despontin, M., Immelman, J., Richter, J., Couto, A., Fitts, L. A., Gao, Y., &
> Dionizio, E. (in press). *Data for deforestation- and conversion-free (DCF)
> supply chain analyses: Applied learnings from soy in Brazil (Guidebook).*
> World Resources Institute.
> [Final citation and DOI to be added on publication.]

## Licence

The **source code** is released under the MIT Licence.

The generated figures, the documentation, the `citation/*.md` text, and
`keyword_dictionary_v1.1.csv` are released under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International Licence (CC BY 4.0): share and adapt freely, **provided you
cite the tool and publication above**.

The default canonical keyword registry (`keyword_dictionary_v1.3.csv`) and the
protocol specification are **adapted from the World Resources Institute guidebook
cited above**. Their licence is **pending** confirmation of the guidebook's own
licence on publication — see `prisma_s/data/DATA_LICENSE.md`.

## Data & attribution policy

This project follows the World Resources Institute data policies:

- Open Data Commitment — https://www.wri.org/data/open-data-commitment
- Permissions & Licensing — https://www.wri.org/research/permissions-licensing
- Data Platforms Terms of Service — https://www.wri.org/about/wri-data-platforms-tos

Where WRI material is used under CC BY 4.0, attribution credits the work as shown
on its wri.org page, links back to that page, and **states that modifications
were made**. Datasets named in the guidebook may carry their own per-dataset
terms (some non-commercial); check each dataset's own licence before reuse.
